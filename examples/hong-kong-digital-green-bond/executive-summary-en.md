# Executive Summary: Hong Kong Digital Green Bonds and Korean Adoption

> Analysis date: 16 November 2026 · Not legal or investment advice

Hong Kong's 2024 digital green bond offering is significant because it joined a digital-native issuance platform to established market infrastructure. The approximately HK$6 billion equivalent transaction covered HKD, RMB, USD and EUR tranches, used HSBC Orion as the digital-assets platform, and used the Central Moneymarkets Unit as its clearing and settlement system. International investors could access the bonds through CMU links to Euroclear and Clearstream. [EV-HKMA-DGB-2024]

The model did not remove securities regulation or traditional intermediaries. Hong Kong's Securities and Futures Commission states that existing legal and regulatory requirements for traditional securities markets continue to apply to tokenised securities. [EV-HK-SFC-TOKEN-2023]

Korea's amended Electronic Securities Act was promulgated but was not yet effective at the analysis date; it is scheduled to take effect on 4 February 2027. The FSC's first-stage policy roadmap prioritises privately placed bonds for institutional investors. [EV-KR-ESA-21318][EV-KR-FSC-POLICY-20260904]

Accordingly, a practical Korean first-stage model would use an institutional KRW private bond, permissioned ledger infrastructure connected to the Korea Securities Depository, and established securities-account and cash-settlement rails. Multi-currency distribution, public offerings and on-chain cash settlement should be treated as later-stage options. The relevant Korean subordinate rules were still drafts and must be checked again before implementation. [EV-KR-FSC-DRAFT-20261001]
